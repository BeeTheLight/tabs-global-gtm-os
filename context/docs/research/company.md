## Modules and features

Homepage product menu: "Payouts", "Collections", "Global accounts", "Coverage". Developer menu: "Documentation", "API reference", "Sandbox", "Status". Source: https://tabsglobal.co/

From the docs (https://docs.tabsglobal.co and https://docs.tabsglobal.co/llms.txt):
- Multi-currency wallets: "Manage real-time balances across 10+ fiat currencies and USD stablecoins (USDT, USDC)."
- Dedicated virtual accounts: "Issue dedicated Nigerian virtual accounts to collect funds locally via NIP bank transfers." (NUBAN accounts)
- Global payouts and transfers: instant commercial bank transfers, account name verification, international wires.
- Guaranteed FX: live rates plus quotes with a 60-second validity window (guaranteed, forward and inverse quote endpoints).
- Crypto deposits: multi-chain deposit addresses settled into merchant balances.
- Beneficiaries, corridor requirements, transaction ledger and receipts endpoints.
- Idempotency keys; signed webhooks (HMAC SHA-256) with retries; sandbox; merchant API keys with live and test environments.
- "Tabs Connect supports collections in 10 currencies across African local currencies, G3 currencies, and stablecoins."

Global accounts (homepage FAQ): "African businesses can hold USD, EUR and GBP accounts with FX built in." Receive via ACH, wire, SWIFT (USD), SEPA and SEPA Instant (EUR), Faster Payments (GBP). Source: https://tabsglobal.co/
