## PROXY (Verto): public review data (Trustpilot, via treg crawl4ai.trustpilot.business.reviews)

- TrustScore 4.4 from 829 reviews; 10 newest read on 2026-11-13. Source: https://www.trustpilot.com/review/verto.co
- Positive themes: fast support, quick transactions. Example 5-star (Oluwadamilare Akanni, NG, 2026-11-13): "The response and engagement is swift."
- Negative themes in the 10 read (4 of 10 were 1 star): onboarding delays and silence. Example 1-star (2026-10-15, US startup): "We submitted our verified US entity application on September 29, 2026 ... their system promising an estimated decision by October 5. Today is October 15". Example (UK limited company, 2026-10-09): "I raised two support tickets, and both were closed with the same response that my query had been escalated to the onboarding team." Example (CA, 2026-09-24): "Verto has confirmed that they have my money, yet I still don't have access to it".
- Reviewer names are in the source; omit them in any copy.
