## The two named incumbents (proxies for Tabs)

### Verto (verto.co, formerly vertofx.com)
Note: verto.com is an unrelated site (a Darwin quote and cookie banner). Use verto.co.
- Self-description: "Cross border payment solutions for global businesses". Products: Business Accounts, FX (49 currencies), Corporate Cards, Verto Atlas (embedded API). Source: https://verto.co/
- Atlas targets "fintechs (PSPs, banks)" and "platforms (marketplaces, SaaS, expense management, brokers)": local collection accounts, 24/7 conversion via API, payments to 100+ countries, mass payouts to African bank accounts and mobile money, webhooks. Local collection currencies: NGN, KES, TZS, AED, XOF, XAF, GHS, RWF, ZAR, DKK, UGX, USD, GBP, EUR (page is inconsistent on which). Atlas Suite launch 2025-08-27. Sources: https://verto.co/atlas/ and https://verto.co/about-us/resources/blog/article/verto-launches-the-atlas-suite/
- Founded 2018 by Anthony Oduu and Ola Oyetayo; London; LinkedIn 201-500 employees. Sources: https://techcrunch.com/2023/04/05/verto-claims-a-quarter-of-svb-customers-operating-in-africa-are-opening-accounts-on-its-platform and https://www.linkedin.com/company/vertofx
- Funding: $10M Series A (2021); "over $12 million" raised per TechCrunch 2023; no confirmed round in 2025 or 2026 (search summary). Source: https://ibsintelligence.com/ibsi-news/verto-raises-10m-in-a-series-a-funding-round-for-cross-border-payments/
- Recent moves: USD business accounts for US to Africa flows, 2026-04-08 to 2026-04-16 (https://verto.co/about-us/resources/blog/article/verto-expands-business-accounts- enable-seamless-payments -from-us-africa/); UAE launch (2025-10); Lagos office (2026-09-01, https://techeconomy.ng/verto-opens-lagos-office-fintech-cross-border-payments); Visa multi-currency card (https://www.techeconomy.ng/verto-launches-new-global-multi-currency-card-in-partnership-with-visa).
- Pricing: see company.md. Trustpilot 4.4 from 829 reviews; recent 1-star themes are onboarding delays and unanswered tickets (see customers.md).
- Hiring (treg blitzapi.jobs.company, 160 results, newest 15 read): Growth and Marketing Manager roles in Dubai and Cape Town, Reconciliation Analyst in Nairobi, Finance Manager in Malta, People Partner in Pune, Talent Partners in London. Some results were for unrelated "Verto" employers (Australian employment services), so the list is noisy. Source: https://www.linkedin.com/company/vertofx (via treg)

### WeWire (wewire.com)
- Self-description: "Virtual Business Accounts & Cross-Border Payment APIs for Global Businesses". Products: Payment APIs, Virtual Accounts, Multi-Currency Wallet, Invoicing, OTC Desk, Stablecoin, Treasury. Source: https://www.wewire.com/
- Scale claims: "3,000+ businesses", "$3b" volume, "100+ countries", ISO 27001. Source: https://www.wewire.com/
- Ghana-founded; CEO Eben Ghanney; Mauritius Global Treasury Activities Licence announced 2025-06-09 ("allowing corporations to hedge their FX risk and access trade finance, local currency liquidity lines, and so much more"). Source: https://blog.wewire.com/product-updates/wewire-secures-mauritius-treasury-license-to-power-trade-finance-innovation/
- Pricing: see company.md (published fee table).
- Funding: Not found.

## Comparison data points found

- Cost of sending $200 across African corridors: about 7.9%, as cited in Nomba's launch coverage. Source: https://techpoint.africa/brandpress/nomba-launches-global-payout-api/ (search summary of the launch pitch).
- Corporate fees for international payments in Africa "average around 1%" and bank wires take 3 to 7 business days, both from vendor-adjacent 2026 guides. Source: https://www.photonpay.com/ca/blog/article/africa-b2b-payment (search summary, not read).
- No independent side-by-side API comparison found.
- Tabs positions itself against none of these by name on its site. Source: https://tabsglobal.co/
- INFERRED: Verto and WeWire both lead with business accounts plus FX; Tabs leads with payouts and collections via one API and adds global accounts. The Tabs docs name only Nigeria, whereas both incumbents publish multi-country coverage.
