# Competitors: payment solutions and APIs for African businesses

Raw research, not conclusions. Date checked for every item: 2026-11-13. I read "APUs" in the brief as APIs. Quotes are short exact quotes from each company's own homepage, captured through a fetch tool. This is a first pass of seven names, not a complete map.

## Nomba
- Hero: "Banking that moves your business forward." Sub: "Accept payments, manage multi currency accounts, and access overdrafts." Source: https://nomba.com
- Cross-border product: Global Payout API, launched March 18, 2026. Lets Nigerian payment firms and businesses collect in naira or stablecoins (USDT, USDC) and pay out abroad in one flow; 5-minute rate lock; corridors UK (Faster Payments), Europe (SEPA), Canada (Interac), DR Congo (mobile money or bank), Nigeria. Fees not disclosed. Source: https://techeconomy.ng/nomba-global-payout-api-cross-border-payments-nigeria/
- CEO Yinka Adewale: "Operators collect naira, then go source foreign currency, all while their customers are waiting." Same source.
- Licence: "Licensed by the Central Bank of Nigeria."; banking services by Nombank MFB. Source: https://nomba.com
- Also appears in Tabs' "Trusted by" logos (see customers.md).

## Grey
- Hero: "Inclusive global banking designed just for you." Sub: "Receive, send, exchange, and manage multiple currencies in one app." Source: https://www.grey.co
- Segments: individuals, freelancers, digital nomads, travellers, businesses ("An IBAN for your business needs"). Products: Foreign accounts, Currency exchange, Virtual cards, Money transfer.
- Pricing language: "Zero account & card maintenance fees". Specific fees: Not found.
- Regulation: "fully regulated by Fintrac and Fincen"; "Grey is a financial technology company, not a bank."
- Expanding its payout network: https://www.africanews.com/2026/09/25/grey-expands-african-payout-network-as-cross-border-payments-demand-grows/ (title seen, article not read).
- Also appears in Tabs' "Trusted by" logos.

## Raenest
- Hero: "Receive and spend money globally". Sub: "Get paid from anywhere in global currencies and do more with money." Source: https://www.raenest.com
- Business side: Make Payments, Receive Payments, Global Accounts, Invoices, Cards; "over 11,000 businesses"; accounts in USD, GBP, EUR, USDT, USDC.
- Licences stated: CBN International Money Transfer Operator; FINTRAC MSB; US MSB; UK services via Clear Junction (FCA EMI).
- Fees: "determined on a case-by-case basis"; amounts Not found.

## Kora
- Hero: "The infrastructure for borderless payments". Sub: "The global fiat and stablecoin engine powering successful transactions." Source: https://www.korahq.com/
- Products: Pay-ins, Payouts, Bulk and Multicurrency Payouts, Virtual Accounts, Global Accounts, Treasury, Stablecoin rails, KYB verification, Payment APIs.
- Countries in selector: Nigeria, Cameroon, Ghana, South Africa, Kenya, Egypt, Ivory Coast. Claims "Local rails in 60+ countries" and "Send funds to 130+ countries in minutes."
- Licences: "Kora or its affiliates provide services under a license or registration in various jurisdictions."; names and numbers Not found on the page.

## Flutterwave
- Hero: "Endless possibilities for all". Sub: "Empowering businesses of all sizes with seamless payment solutions". Source: https://flutterwave.com
- Products include Collect Payments, Send Money, Store, Payment Links, Invoices, Capital, Card Issuing, FaaS, Send App. Fourteen African countries listed (Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, Egypt, and others).
- "We've secured our banking license in Nigeria, our largest market." Fees: Not found on the page.

## Verto
- Homepage fetch returned only a quote ("It is not the strongest of the species that survive...") and no product copy. Not found from its own site.
- Press: named USD accounts for US businesses paying into Africa, aimed at the US-Africa trade gap. Sources: https://techeconomy.ng/verto-launches-usd-business-accounts/ , https://businessday.ng/technology/article/verto-targets-50bn-us-africa-trade-gap-with-named-dollar-accounts-to-bypass-banking-frictions/ (search summaries only).

## Observations (INFERRED)
- Tabs' homepage and docs lead with developer infrastructure for businesses ("One endpoint per market"), while Grey and Raenest lead with accounts for freelancers and SMBs. Kora and Nomba are the closest on API payouts and global accounts.
- Tabs publishes no fees, licence numbers or named partner banks; Raenest, Grey and Nomba each name regulators.
