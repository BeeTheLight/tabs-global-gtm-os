# Voice: how Tabs Global describes itself

Raw research, not conclusions. Refreshed 2026-11-13; every item checked 2026-11-13. Quotes were captured through a summarising fetch tool, so re-check them against the live page before using in copy. INFERRED means my reading.

## Tabs Global in its own words

Source for all lines in this section unless stated: https://tabsglobal.co/

Headline: "Borderless money movement for Africa"

Taglines and section lines:
- "One balance out to every local rail."
- "Collect the way each market already pays."
- "Infrastructure that already carries volume."
- "Global banking infrastructure for African businesses."
- "One endpoint per market you were going to integrate separately."
- "Start with one market. Keep the same integration."

Product and section names: Payouts, Collections, Global accounts, Coverage. Developer names: Documentation, API reference, Sandbox, Status. API product: Tabs Connect, described in the docs as a "bank-grade money movement platform for global businesses" (as captured in the earlier version of this research; the 2026-11-13 docs index confirms the name "Tabs Developer Documentation" only). Use-case tags: Global trade, Supplier payments, Financial software.

Navigation: Solutions, Products, Resources, API docs, Blog, FAQs, Careers, Sign in, Get a demo.

Stats strip: "$100m+" processed, "<5 min" settlement, "99.9%" uptime, "20+" currencies (claims, unverified; see company.md).

FAQ questions (exact):
- "What is Tabs?"
- "Which African countries and payment rails does Tabs support?"
- "How fast are Tabs payouts?"
- "How do I integrate with Tabs?"
- "Can businesses collect payments in local currencies?"
- "Does Tabs offer USD, EUR and GBP accounts?"
- "How does Tabs handle compliance?"
- "How do I get started with Tabs?"

FAQ answer lines (exact where quoted): "Tabs is a cross-border payments platform that moves money into and out of Africa." Compliance checks run inside the payment flow and "Account names are verified on the rail before money moves." Most local payouts settle "Instant · 24/7"; South Africa follows bank hours.

Social and registry copy:
- LinkedIn About: "Tabs is the payment infrastructure for Africa. We enable global businesses move money seamlessly across the continent." Source: https://www.linkedin.com/company/tabsglobal
- X bio: "Payment infrastructure for Africa". Source: https://x.com/TabsNetwork
- Legal self-description: "Tabs Global Limited is a financial technology company, not a bank." Source: https://tabsglobal.co/compliance
- Docs voice (developer-facing): "Prevent duplicate charges and payouts" for idempotency (from the earlier version of this research, not re-checked), "Institutional FX quotes with a 60-second validity window", "Bank-grade idempotency for payouts and charges". Source: https://docs.tabsglobal.co/llms.txt

Patterns, INFERRED: short declaratives with full stops; "one" repeated (one balance, one endpoint, one integration); infrastructure vocabulary (rails, endpoint, balance, settlement); no pricing, no customer quotes, no first-person voice from a named person.

Not found: founder voice, blog posts, case-study language, LinkedIn posts, any named spokesperson.

## Comparison, INFERRED

- Tabs is terser and more infrastructure-led than both incumbents, which lead with accounts and cost savings.
- Both incumbents quote percentages or ratings (Verto "40% reduction in cross-border payment costs", WeWire "Save over 50% in transaction fees compared to traditional bank wires" for stablecoin) while Tabs shows only a 0.01% demo fee. Sources: https://verto.co/ and https://www.wewire.com/
