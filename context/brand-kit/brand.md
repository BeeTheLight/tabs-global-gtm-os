## Tone (INFERRED)
- Technical and product-led; short declarative sentences.
- Confident and plain: states what the product does, not why you should feel anything.
- Names rails and mechanics (NIP, SEPA, webhooks) rather than abstract benefits.
- Light on adjectives; "bank-grade" and "institutional" are the strongest ones used.
