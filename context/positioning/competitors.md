## Where Tabs Global wins (INFERRED)
- Developer-first: documented API, sandbox, idempotency, signed webhooks, "Every rail, no contract" (confirmed on the homepage 2026-11-13). [company], [voice]
- Business-only focus, versus Grey and Raenest consumer lines. [company], [competitors]
- Compliance in the flow: homepage FAQ says "KYB, screening and transaction monitoring run inside the payment flow, with audit trails included." and "Account names are verified on the rail before money moves." [voice]
- Start small: "Start with one market. Keep the same integration." [voice]
- Possible speed-of-onboarding angle, INFERRED from incumbent complaints; Tabs' own onboarding time is Not found. [customers], [signals]

## Where Tabs Global loses (INFERRED)
- Proof: no named customers, case studies or scale evidence; Verto names customers and publishes stories, and WeWire names logos. Tabs is new (2-10 employees, founded 2026). [customers], [company]
- Coverage claims: Kora says 60+ countries; Verto says 190+ countries for receiving and 8 regulated jurisdictions; Tabs says six live and 14 in rollout, and its docs name only Nigeria. [competitors], [company]
- Licence transparency: Verto names an FCA firm reference; WeWire names FINTRAC and Mauritius licences; Raenest, Nomba and Flutterwave name licences; Tabs publishes none. [company], [competitors]
- Breadth: no cards or credit listed; Verto lists corporate cards, Flutterwave card issuing and capital, Nomba overdrafts, WeWire an OTC desk. [company], [competitors]
- Pricing: no published fees, while WeWire publishes a fee table and Verto publishes plans. [company], [competitors]
- Security certification: both incumbents claim ISO 27001; Tabs claims none. [company], [competitors]

## How to respond
- "Why not Verto or WeWire?" Compare on the integration experience and the single endpoint. Do not claim more countries, lower fees or faster onboarding; none is supported.
- "Why not Nomba or Kora?" Same rule.
- "Who else uses you?" Only what is approved. Nomba, Paystack and Grey appear as "Trusted by" logos but are unconfirmed. [customers]
- "Are you licensed?" Quote only the verified wording: services run through "licensed banks and payment institutions that Tabs partners with" (compliance page, 2026-11-13). Do not name banks or regulators. [company]
- "What does it cost?" Pricing is confirmed at transaction time per the Terms; do not quote 0.01%. [company]
- Never name a competitor or quote competitor complaints in prospect copy.
- Open question: win and loss reasons from real deals. Not found.
