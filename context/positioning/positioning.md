## Positioning statement
For businesses that move money into and out of Africa, Tabs Global is a cross-border payments platform that gives one API and one balance across local rails and USD, EUR and GBP accounts, with screening built into each payment, so teams can start with one market and keep the same integration as they add more. Unlike running a separate integration per market, Tabs routes each payout to the local rail from a single endpoint. (Statement is assembled from Tabs' own copy; its claims are self-reported and unverified.)
